02 — CHECK IT

Tally shows its working.

It reads every line, including discounts, SST, service charge and 5-sen rounding, then checks the items add up to the total. If a line looks off, Tally points at it so you can fix it. Fix one, and it remembers next time.

04 — RELIEFS

Tax season, without the shoebox of receipts.

Tally flags buys that may count for LHDN relief, from the receipt’s words. Wrong? Mark it “Not a tax relief”, or pick the one it missed. Zakat and donations are listed apart. Relief receipt photos stay 7 years, ready to download. Check LHDN’s limits.

Before you buy

Can I afford it? Ask before, not after.

Type a price. Tally walks your next 30 days (money now, pay, bills, usual spending) and judges by your lowest day, not the month’s end. Short before payday means “tight”, even if pay fixes it. Few entries yet? It says it’s a rough guess.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Still unsure? Tally is open source, so you can read the code on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no premium tier and no ads. It’s a passion project, and because your phone does the work there are no servers to pay for.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone. There’s no account. Receipts are read on the device and never uploaded. If you back up, it goes to your own Google Drive or a file, only when you choose.

Can I delete my receipt photos?

Yes. In Settings → Privacy → Receipt photos, delete them all now, or let Tally delete photos older than 30 days, 90 days or a year. The entry stays: shop, date, amount and items. When Tally tidies by age, it keeps photos of possible tax-relief buys for the 7 years LHDN can ask for them. Deleting all removes those too, your choice.

Does Tally read my SMS or need my bank login?

No. Tally never reads your SMS or OTPs, and never asks for your bank login, card or PIN. The only part of your phone it asks to use is the camera, to snap receipts, and you can say no and type instead. Bank records come in only when you pick a statement file yourself.

Why does it need the internet?

To open the first time, to download the receipt reader once (about 30 MB), and to update itself. After that it works offline. Otherwise it goes online only when you ask: a Google Sheets link you paste, an exchange rate you ask for, feedback you send, or a calendar reminder you add. Your entries and receipt photos are never sent.

Setting it up for a parent?

Open tallymy.github.io on their phone, pick their language and a bigger text size, then add it to the home screen. Choose “Simple” in Settings → Features for just the basics. There’s no account or password for them to remember, and their entries stay on their phone.

Will it read my receipts?

It copes with faded, crumpled, far-away and sideways photos, and recognises over 3,000 Malaysian chains and shop names. It won’t be right every time, which is why it checks the items add up to the total and points at any line worth a second look. Fix one and Tally remembers it next time.

Is it a real app if I install it from a browser?

It’s a web app that installs to your home screen, opens like an app and works offline once opened.

What if I change phones?

In Settings, Backup & restore makes a backup file. Send it to yourself (Google Drive, email, WhatsApp), then restore it on the new phone. There’s no account, so back up before you switch.

Is the tax relief part tax advice?

No. Tally flags receipts and items that may count as relief candidates and keeps the receipt photo with each. It doesn’t know your circumstances or work out what you can claim; each relief has limits and conditions. It’s an estimate to help you prepare. Please check LHDN’s rules for what you can claim.

Who makes it?

One independent developer in Malaysia. It’s open source (MIT), so you can read the code on GitHub. Feedback or a bug? Open an issue on GitHub.

What if I stop using it, or it stops being updated?

Your data is on your phone, and you can make a backup file whenever you like. The code is open source (MIT) on GitHub, so it isn’t locked away.
